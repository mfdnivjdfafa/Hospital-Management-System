Hospital Management System (HMS)

Project Overview

The Hospital Management System (HMS) is a software project designed to manage hospital activities digitally. It aims to reduce paperwork, organize patient information, and improve the management of appointments, billing, pharmacy inventory, and medical records.

Objectives

To maintain patient records digitally.

To manage appointments efficiently.

To simplify billing and payment records.

To track medicine stock and availability.

To improve access to hospital information.

To reduce manual work and paperwork.

Key Features

Patient Management: Patient registration and medical history.

Appointment Scheduling: Booking, viewing, rescheduling, and cancelling appointments.

Billing and Payments: Managing bills and recording payments.

Pharmacy and Inventory: Tracking medicine stock and identifying low-stock items.

Medical Records: Maintaining patient history for authorized hospital staff.

Project Scope

The system focuses on core hospital operations, including patient registration, appointment scheduling, billing, medical records, and pharmacy inventory management.
